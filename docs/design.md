# Visual and motion specification
## Identity
The original logo has a lighter indigo/violet character than the printed card mockup. Use the original as master.
Sampled from frequent solid pixels in the uploaded raster:
- Main indigo #31326F
- Secondary blue #2D5090
- Violet #6E57A0
The raster has antialiasing and slightly varying shades; these are practical web tokens sampled from this file, not a claim about a vector brand standard.
Supporting design colours: ink #15162F, warm white #F6F5F2, pure white #FFFFFF, soft neutral #E5E4EA.
Use violet in small accents. The full logo remains unchanged.

The standalone logo reads "On Time Every Time." Keep that exact public wording and punctuation.
The final card has an old tagline in its faint watermark; do not use that watermark or extract the logo from the card.
The PNG has substantial white margins. Present the intact artwork in a white header/brand area; a non-destructive CSS viewport may exclude exterior empty margins but must never clip the horse, stripes, registration mark or tagline. Preserve aspect ratio. Do not apply colour filters.

## Composition
Aim for a distinct editorial automotive website with precise corporate service information.
Header: white, crisp full logo, Services / Fleet / About / Contact, strong enquiry action.
Hero: use public/images/india/hero.png. Place copy in the darker left space on desktop; protect readability with a subtle dark overlay. On mobile use a deliberate image crop or separate image region so the subject and text stay clear.
Eyebrow: VICTOR MOBILITY · INDIA
Hero headline: On Time Every Time.
Support: Employee transportation, airport transfers and event travel—with vehicles for everyday journeys and special occasions.
Primary CTA: Discuss your requirement
Secondary CTA: Explore our fleet
Under hero: service names as clear text navigation; no unverified counters.
Next: employee-transport feature with the bus photo and concrete copy.
Fleet: vehicle-category selector with readable details, not an auto-rotating carousel.
Then event/airport/luxury pathways, cities, concise company story and final contact invitation.
Vary section structure. Avoid repeating six identical icon boxes, oversized pill buttons, floating abstract shapes and neon gradients.

Start with one readable sans family such as Manrope, using weight and scale carefully. The brand wordmark itself is never typeset with a substitute font.
Generous spacing with clear alignment. Body copy around 16–18 px. Target at least 44 px interactive hit areas as a project preference.

## Motion
Hero: short fade/settle, roughly 650 ms. Do not make text unusable or wait for an intro.
Sections: 16–24 px vertical reveal over 450–600 ms, once.
Fleet change: coordinated image/details transition around 250 ms.
Menu: around 200 ms, keyboard and Escape supported.
Buttons: 120–180 ms state changes.
Keep standard scrolling and pointer. Use transforms/opacity thoughtfully. No perpetual bouncing or spinning.
Reduced motion: remove parallax and large movement; content is immediately readable.
Use a still hero for version one. Avoid adding an autoplay video requirement to the four-day critical path.

## Imagery
Three generated illustrative assets are supplied in public/images/india. They are not actual fleet documentation.
Use a discreet "Vehicle imagery is illustrative." caption in relevant fleet/media context.
Never caption them as real Victor vehicles or name an exact model based on a generated appearance.
See media.json for paths and focal points. Do not expose internal asset manifests to visitors.

## Mobile and review
Review at 390 px and 1440 px immediately; also inspect a smaller phone size before launch.
No clipped headline, horizontal overflow, low-contrast thin text, hidden nav focus or hover-only content.
The static screenshot must look intentional before decorative animation is added.
